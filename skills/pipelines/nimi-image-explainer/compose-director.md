# Nimi image explainer: compose

Require the approved scene plan and asset checkpoint. Use the App's bounded media worker with AudioMixer and VideoCompose. Remotion is the selected renderer. Use actual narration durations; planning timestamps do not become measured timing.

Stage media and the prebuilt composition outside the installed payload. Record failure when the worker fails or output cannot be stored. A successful `render_report` refers to the real stored video and measured dimensions/duration. Provide playback and export. Decode checks do not establish subjective audio quality, and a running process is not a completed render.
