# OpenMontage

Produce narrated videos from source footage and generated media. Review the idea, script and scene plan, approve the material, then inspect and deliver the final video.

## Make a video

Open OpenMontage from Nimi Home and configure the capabilities needed by your selected workflow. Start with a quick explainer or the available hybrid workflow, provide a topic or footage, and review the proposed script and scene plan.

Generate or select the material you want to use. Confirm the material approval before composition. Review the finished video before using the delivery action; video and subtitle downloads have separate actions.

Current output uses a 1280 × 720 canvas. Planning durations are targets; actual duration follows the generated media and approved edit. Transcription results and manual or paragraph subtitles need review. Automatic word alignment, multilingual dubbing and some specialized workflows remain unfinished; a catalogue entry alone does not establish an available workflow.

## Web preview

The Web preview currently uses admitted local development sources. Keep Nimi Home and OpenMontage open on the PC. Sign in with the same Nimi account, confirm the source in Home, and allow its media operations under Integrations. Android adaptation is still being verified; there is no public Web or Android release entry.

PC, Web and Android registrations keep separate project stores. Existing PC projects stay in their original registration. AI runs on the connected Runtime; composition and audio preparation run in the PC App.

If a connection is lost during composition, reconnect and choose **读取原合成结果** to check the original operation. The App does not automatically resubmit it. Keep the PC App open until the result is saved; closing it can make an uncollected media result unavailable. Previously saved project assets and exports remain. Individual source/preview media is limited to 128 MiB; combined composition inputs are limited to 64 MiB.

## Projects and costs

Projects, checkpoints and generated media use Nimi storage. Changing a plan can invalidate downstream material. Uncertain submissions pause rather than blindly repeating generation. Check task state before retrying or assuming a stop has completed.

Cloud AI resources receive the inputs required by the selected task and may incur service charges. Costs can be unavailable in the interface. Keep Nimi running while working, and export important finished work.

For help, contact the App provider with the workflow and error details, excluding private source footage.
